## Design Context

### Users
Lewis. Desk, phone in hand. He already has the formula. He wants to slam size into a prediction-market event and feel the tote walk.

### Brand Personality
Dense, unimpressed, mechanical. Clerk, not marketer.

### Aesthetic Direction
Off-course tote board. Tobacco field, amber LED cents, one cream betting slip. Chicago industrial type plus a newsprint serif for the question. Not Polymarket purple, not Kalshi blue, not neon crypto.

### Design Principles
1. The cents are the product.
2. One physical object: the slip. The board is not a card grid.
3. Always show the walk: first slice versus last slice.
4. Phone first: prices, slip, rails. Knobs under a fold.
5. Teach by doing.
